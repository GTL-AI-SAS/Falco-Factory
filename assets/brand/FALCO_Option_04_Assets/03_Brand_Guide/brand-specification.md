# FALCO — Brand identity quick guide

**Parent brand:** GTL AI  
**Selected direction:** Vector Beak (final identity system / option 04 reference)  
**Product role:** Reactive, customer-facing AI assistant for business websites and customer workflows.

## Brand idea

A geometric falcon profile framed by an open circular stroke. The falcon communicates intelligence, responsiveness and precision; the open ring connects the mark to the GTL AI visual family without copying the parent 夢 emblem.

## Core palette

| Token | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Near Black | `#090B0F` | Primary dark surfaces |
| Mint / turquoise | `#68E2C3` | Emblem ring, the final **C** in the FALCO wordmark, and brand accent |
| Off White | `#F3F4F6` | Primary logo and text on dark |
| Graphite | `#2B313A` | Secondary dark surfaces |

## Signature wordmark accent

The final **C** in every FALCO wordmark is the Mint / turquoise accent (`#68E2C3`), matching the open emblem ring and the final cinematic identity animation.

- Keep **FAL** and **O** in the foreground color defined by the selected logo variant.
- Apply the Mint accent to the **C** consistently across horizontal, stacked, compact, and standalone wordmark assets.
- The all-mint wordmark variant remains a single-color option; emblem-only app icons and favicon assets are unchanged.
- This is a fixed brand treatment, not a random recoloring effect.

## Typography

- **Primary recommendation:** Inter Display ExtraBold / Bold for the FALCO wordmark.
- **UI and supporting text:** Inter.
- Keep the wordmark uppercase, bold and compact. Avoid artificial outlines, bevels or shadows.

## Logo compositions

- **Primary horizontal:** emblem left, FALCO wordmark right.
- **Stacked:** emblem above wordmark.
- **Compact horizontal:** reduced emblem and wordmark for tight UI headers.
- **Standalone emblem:** app icon, favicon, avatar and small product marks.

## Clear space

Use at least one emblem-stroke width of clear space around the standalone mark. For lockups, keep a minimum gap equal to the emblem's ring stroke between emblem and wordmark.

## Minimum sizes

- Emblem: 24 px recommended for UI; 16 px only for favicon use.
- Horizontal lockup: 150 px wide recommended.
- App icon: 32 px favicon; 180 px Apple touch icon; 512 px app-store / product icon.

## Usage

- Preserve original proportions and spacing.
- Use approved palette variants only.
- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, add glow/shadow effects, or place the dark logo on low-contrast surfaces.
- Use emblem-only monochrome versions when color reproduction is unavailable.
- Wordmark lockups retain the Mint **C** accent in their black and white foreground variants; the existing `Monochrome/` file paths are retained for compatibility.

## Asset note

The SVGs contain editable vector paths for the emblem and editable text for the wordmark. The PNG exports are raster renderings of those SVG assets. The logo geometry is a clean vector reconstruction of the selected generated concept board, prepared as a practical starter asset set; it is not an automatically traced extraction from the presentation image.
